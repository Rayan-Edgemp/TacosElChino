From the Food Truck to Midtown Reno 🌮

A new chapter on South Virginia Street.

A New Home in Midtown 💐

The official Tacos El Chino profiles connect the Cocina & Bar directly with the Tacos El Chino food truck in Reno. In May 2026, the brand opened its brick-and-mortar restaurant at 1553 S Virginia Street, adding a Midtown dining room and bar.

Rooted in Reno 💚

The Cocina & Bar is connected to the Tacos El Chino food truck on Kietzke Lane. Together, the two locations give guests a food-truck experience and a full dine-in restaurant and bar setting in Reno.

Food, Drinks and Community ✨

The Midtown restaurant has promoted Taco Tuesday, margaritas, micheladas, beer, family-friendly dining, sports watch parties and occasional community events.
