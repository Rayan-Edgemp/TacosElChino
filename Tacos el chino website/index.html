Welcome to Tacos El Chino Cocina & Bar

Located at 1553 S Virginia Street in Reno, Tacos El Chino Cocina & Bar opened its Midtown restaurant in May 2026. The restaurant is officially connected to the Tacos El Chino Reno food truck, bringing the name into a full dine-in restaurant and bar setting.

From a Reno Food Truck to Midtown

Tacos El Chino now has two connected ways to find the brand in Reno: the Cocina & Bar on South Virginia Street and the Tacos El Chino food truck on Kietzke Lane. The Midtown restaurant adds dine-in service, drinks and occasional community events.
